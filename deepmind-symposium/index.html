BENGALURU · 29–30 OCTOBER 2026

Two days of ideas,
and the people around them.

I was selected for a fully funded place at the Google DeepMind APAC Research Symposium 2026. The sessions were excellent; the conversations, food, and stay made it feel like a whole experience.

Google DeepMindAPAC Research SymposiumFully funded

WHAT STAYED WITH ME

Good talks. Better conversations.

At the Google office, I especially enjoyed Dr. Aditya’s session on Matryoshka, Dr. Mausam’s session on AI in material science and pathology, and Dr. Amitesh’s session on AI safety. Each left me with something to think about beyond the room.

The networking sessions were just as memorable: meeting people working on very different questions made the research feel connected to a wider community. The food and stay were lovely too, and are very much part of the memory.
