THE WORK & THE TAKEAWAY

Learning what pulls you in is part of the work.

At IIT Mandi, I explored how machine learning could be applied to 3D propellers and geometry, and carried out a literature review on ML for amphibian propellers. It was a valuable engineering challenge, but I soon realized that ML for 3D geometry wasn’t the direction I felt most drawn to 🙂.

That clarity mattered. My later work with multimodal clinical data and graph learning helped me find my way toward machine learning and core deep learning—the areas I’m excited to keep exploring.
